Little Keeper — Captain (character artwork)

Source: "参考图 1 · Captain 角色视觉标准" (Captain Character Reference), provided
by the Little Days product owner on 2026-11-10 as the only art standard for
Captain and authorised for use in Little Days. Captain is based on the owner's
own grey cat, Captain.

Files (cut out with tools/extract_captain.py; nothing redrawn, every pixel is
from the reference; the reference itself is not shipped):
- LD-keeper-cat-lemon-normal.png  "Normal / 默认"   197x251 canvas (shared)
- LD-keeper-cat-lemon-happy.png   "Happy / 开心"    (sparkles removed)
- LD-keeper-cat-lemon-sleepy.png  "Sleepy / 睡觉"   ("zZ" removed)
- LD-keeper-cat-lemon-peek.png    "Peek / 探头"     339x290 (reference card removed)
The flat background and the baked drop shadow are removed; the app adds its
own soft shadow.

Second picture (V10.8, the "yarn ball" look): "Captain with a ball of yarn",
no hat (docs/brand/LD-captain-natural-yarn-original.png, 941x1672), provided
by the product owner. Cut out with tools/extract_captain_look.py; nothing
redrawn. The backdrop, floor shadow, heart and sparkles are removed; the yarn
and its thread stay.
- LD-keeper-cat-yarn-happy.png    one pose, 320 px wide
On a dark page a faint light edge can remain under the paws (the picture's
own floor shadow); the app's pages are light.

Usage: inside Little Days only. Not for redistribution or resale.

Wanted looks that have NO artwork yet (they are not in the app and are never
drawn in code): flower clip, knit hat, frog hat, sleep cap; scarf, pencil,
apron. Each needs approved pictures of Captain wearing it — at least the
"Normal" pose (transparent or flat background, about 600 px tall), ideally
also Happy, Sleepy and Peek, in the same style and scale as the reference.

Still needed from the artist: higher-resolution renders of each state
(the reference figures are only ~190 px wide, the Peek ~340 px), and
consistent animation frames if blinking or other motion is wanted.
