Capiday background music (LD-music-*.mp3)

Original music composed for the Capiday project. Every note, drum and
texture (piano, ukulele, upright bass, brushes, rain, waves, café murmur,
vinyl crackle) is synthesised from code in
tools/make_music.py; no samples, recordings, loops or songs by anyone else are
used, and nothing was taken from a streaming service. The chord progressions
are common ones; the melodies were written for this project.

Users may play these pieces in the app and include them in the photos,
videos and books they make from their own journals, including when sharing
them with family and friends.
